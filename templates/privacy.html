Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how your financial data stays yours.

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly ("we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly you agree to the practices described here.

01Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and a securely hashed version of your password.
  • Expense data: the amounts, categories, dates and descriptions you enter.
  • Technical data: basic information needed to keep you signed in, such as a session cookie.

We do not connect to your bank and never ask for card or account numbers.

02How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Create and secure your account.
  • Store your expenses and show you summaries and breakdowns of your spending.
  • Maintain and improve the reliability of the service.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

03Sharing of Information

Your expenses are private to your account. We do not share your personal information with third parties, except where required by law.

04Cookies

Spendly uses a session cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

05Data Security & Retention

Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account exists. No method of storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

06Your Choices

You can view, edit or delete your expenses at any time and update your profile details from your account.

07Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continuing to use Spendly after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.
